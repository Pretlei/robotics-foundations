Lesson: it's almost impossible to get perfect data through cameras due to external influences. It's cluttered
noisy, incomplete, distorted measurements, the degree of which depends on the quality of the camera.
Filters and multiple sources of information (sensors) must be used to increase accuracy, but it's never 100%
because they're trade-offs, not solutions.
This is one of the reasons why robots aren't as trustworthy as people want them to be. They have to make 
reliable decisions from unreliable data. They lack the common sense that humans have, but AI has the potential 
to solve that issue.
